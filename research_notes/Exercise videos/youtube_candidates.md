# YouTube demonstration videos for Note by Note exercises

Research date: 1 October 2026. Exercise wording is taken from `js/lessons.js` (LESSONS, UNITS, MOVE_CARDS, CONTROLS, buildWarmup). No app files were changed.

## How these were checked (read this first)

- **YouTube was not reachable from this session.** The network proxy blocks youtube.com, the oEmbed endpoint, noembed, Invidious and Piped mirrors, and also most teacher websites (djarts.com.au, singup.org, carnegiehall.org). So I could not open any video, check its length, or watch it.
- **What I did confirm.** Every URL below came back from a web-search index with that exact video ID and title. None are made up. For the channel name, I used the search snippet or the teacher's own website, where that website shows a companion page with the same title (Dr Dan's djarts.com.au "singing tips" pages match his YouTube titles one for one).
- **Status labels used in the tables:**
  - **Indexed**: video ID and title confirmed in a search index, and the channel is confirmed by the snippet or the teacher's own site.
  - **Indexed, channel unverified**: video ID and title confirmed, but the channel was not shown. It is inferred, or left blank.
  - **Unverified**: something I could not confirm. The reason is given.
- **Lengths are unverified throughout.** A length is given only where the title states it ("1 minute", "5 minute"), and it is marked "per title". Anything else says "?". Before adding any video to the app, a parent should watch it once and note the real length and a start time.
- **Start times.** I could not watch the videos, so start times are blank ("–"). Most of the teacher videos below have a 30 to 90 second spoken intro, often with a channel plug, before the exercise. Add `&start=NN` (or `?start=NN` in an embed) once someone has watched the video.

### Age-fit key
- **K**: young children (about 4 to 8). Playful, short, with a child or a children's educator on screen.
- **T**: tweens and teens (about 9 to 16+). Clear explanation, adult presenter, nothing unsuitable.
- **P**: parent-facing. Watch it yourself, then coach the child in your own words.

## Exercise list (what the app actually asks)

| # | App name (where) | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Find your range (Start here) | Sing a low note and a high note; lessons then move into the singer's key. Separate note in app: if your voice is changing (cracks, squeaks, new low notes), redo the range test. |
| 2 | Match a note (Start here) | Hear a note, sing it back on "ah" when the bar reaches the line. 6 notes. |
| 3 | Hold it steady (Start here; also Today's challenge) | Breathe in, then hold each note on "ah" (4, 6 and 8 s). In Today's challenge it is "oo" (5 and 6 s). "Keep the line level to the end." |
| 4 | Step up, step down (Pitch) | Short stepwise patterns (1-2-1, 1-2-3-2-1, 3-2-1) echoed on "ah". |
| 5 | Five-note scale (Pitch; also in Daily warm-up) | 1-2-3-4-5-4-3-2-1 on "mee". Each round starts a little higher. |
| 6 | The full scale (Pitch) | Do re mi fa sol la ti do and back, on "ah" or on the note names. |
| 7 | Leaps (Pitch) | Jumps of a 3rd and a 5th (1-3-1, 1-5-1, 1-3-5-3-1, 1-5-3-1) on "oo". "Hear the landing note in your head before you sing it." |
| 8 | Arpeggios (Pitch) | 1-3-5-8-5-3-1 on "nay". "Keep the top note light." |
| 9 | Minor mood (Pitch) | Minor 5-note scale, then a minor triad, on "ah". The third is a half step lower. |
| 10 | Echo game (Pitch) | New short random tunes (do re mi sol la) to echo on "la". |
| 11 | Longer holds (Breath skills) | 8, 10 and 12 s on one breath, on "oo". |
| 12 | Sirens (Breath skills) | Hum with lips closed, or motorboat lips, sliding up and down in widening rounds. "Gentle, never pushed. Only go as high as feels easy. If tight or scratchy, make the slide smaller, or stop and drink water." |
| 13 | Short and sharp (Breath skills; also Today's challenge "Bouncy 'ha'") | Short bouncy staccato notes on "ha" (1-3-5-3-1). "Let your belly kick each one out, then stop cleanly." |
| 14a | Warm-up move card: Shoulder rolls | Up, back and down, slowly, 3 times. |
| 14b | Warm-up move card: Loose jaw | Rub cheeks in little circles; let the jaw hang loose. |
| 14c | Warm-up move card: Yawn and sigh | Big yawn, then a gentle sigh from high to low. |
| 14d | Warm-up move card: Slow hiss | In through the nose, hiss "sssss" slowly until the air runs out; shoulders stay down. |
| 15 | Hum slides (Daily warm-up) | Small, gentle hum or lip-trill sirens (3 widening rounds). |
| 16 | Light "oo" down (Daily warm-up) | 5-4-3-2-1 on "oo", light and soft, each round a little lower. |
| 17 | Today's challenge: Grow and shrink | Swell on "ah" (6 s): soft, louder, then soft, with the pitch steady. Messa di voce. |
| 18 | Today's challenge: Slow slide down | Slow smooth glide down on "oo", "smooth like a lift, no bumps". |
| 19 | Big siren (Daily warm-up) | One full-width hum siren to finish. "As high as feels easy, and no higher." |
| x | Cross-cutting | Lip trills / "motorboat lips" (the alternative in Sirens and Hum slides). The warm-up intro also says: "Sing gently, at a medium volume; stop and drink water if scratchy." |

Five-note scale (#5) appears both as a lesson and in the warm-up; Hold it steady and Bouncy "ha" reuse #3 and #13. The video sections below follow this list.

## General notes

### Channels that were consistently good
- **Sing Up, "Inside the voice" series** (UK charity, with Dr Jenevora Williams, a leading children's-voice researcher, and Stuart Barr). It is the most credible source and calm and child-safe, with no hard selling. It covers posture and body wake-up, breathing and hiss, tone onset, tongue and larynx release, and resonance. It is aimed at teachers and older children, so it suits the teens directly and the parent for the younger girls.
- **Dr Dan's Voice Essentials** (Dr Dan Robinson, Australia). He has hundreds of short, clean, technique-first videos with a companion page for each on djarts.com.au. They cover sirens, lip trills, onsets, messa di voce, minor triads, jaw release, range and cool-downs. The channel is aimed at adult learners, with gentle product mentions; good for the teens.
- **Carnegie Hall Musical Explorers** (teaching artist Shanna Whitney). This was the best material for the 4 to 8 year olds (sirens as police cars, Hoot Owl head voice, echoing), but the main warm-up video is on **Vimeo**.
- **Voice Lessons To Go** (Ariella Vaccarino). Clear solfege and scale videos.

### Gaps (no good video found)
- Child-friendly **range finding** that avoids voice-type labels.
- **Five-note scale on "mee"** with technique coaching. The Dr Dan 5-note video's YouTube ID is unconfirmed.
- **Light descending "oo"** pattern (#16) and the **slow "oo" slide down** (#18), coached for kids.
- **Grow and shrink (swell)** for children. Existing videos treat messa di voce as advanced.
- **Light top note on "nay" arpeggios**. Most "nay" videos teach bratty or belting sounds; avoid them.
- **Bouncy "ha" staccato** for young kids.
- **Minor vs major** explained by singing, at a child's level.
- For all of these, consider recording a 30 to 60 s parent or teacher clip, or using the app's own spoken prompts (see `research_notes/Voice prompts licensed songs free sing/spoken_guidance_for_pre_readers.md`).

### Practical notes for showing YouTube inside a kids' app
- **Embed with privacy-enhanced mode**: `https://www.youtube-nocookie.com/embed/VIDEO_ID?rel=0&modestbranding=1&start=NN`. Google says this mode keeps embedded views out of the viewer's YouTube history and recommendations, and that any ads served are non-personalised. It **delays** cookies until play but does not stop all data going to Google: the iframe still loads from Google and sends the IP address.
- **Related videos cannot be turned off.** Since September 2018, `rel=0` only limits the end-of-video suggestions to the **same channel**; it no longer hides them. For a kids' app, choose videos from "clean" channels (Sing Up, Carnegie Hall, Dr Dan), or close or hide the player when the video ends. With the IFrame Player API, listen for the `ENDED` state and swap the iframe back to a thumbnail, so the end screen never shows. `start=` and `end=` can cut long intros and trailing promos.
- **Ads.** Monetised videos can show ads in embeds as well. Videos marked "made for kids" get only contextual (non-personalised) ads and have comments, end-screen cards and the miniplayer turned off. Most adult singing-teacher videos are **not** marked "made for kids", so they may show normal ads and links. Sing Up and Carnegie Hall content is less likely to carry ads, but check by watching.
- **Australian under-16 rule.** Since **10 December 2025**, under the Online Safety Amendment (Social Media Minimum Age) Act 2024, YouTube must stop Australians under 16 from **holding accounts**, so under-16s are signed out. They can still **watch while signed out**, and embedded players work without an account, so the app's embeds keep working. Signed out, though, YouTube's supervised-account parental controls no longer apply. **YouTube Kids is exempt**, but it cannot be embedded in another app. No penalty falls on children or parents. Source: YouTube Help, "YouTube access restrictions for users under 16 in Australia" (support.google.com/youtube/answer/16758081) and the OAIC and eSafety pages.
- **Vimeo option.** The Carnegie Hall warm-up is on Vimeo. Vimeo embeds can be set with no related videos (`?dnt=1` for do-not-track), which is cleaner for children.
- **Offline / PWA.** The app has a service worker (`sw.js`) and no Content-Security-Policy today. Embedded videos need a network connection and must not be cached. If a CSP is ever added, it needs `frame-src https://www.youtube-nocookie.com https://player.vimeo.com`. Show a friendly "needs internet" message when offline instead of a broken iframe.
- **Safer alternative.** Rather than embedding, show a "Watch how (parent)" link that opens in the browser. A parent can then check any ads or suggestions first. This works well for the 4 to 8 year olds.
- **Before shipping any video**: watch it end to end and record the real length, a start time that skips the intro, and the end-screen behaviour. Confirm the channel, which I could not do for several videos above. Re-check periodically, because videos get deleted or re-titled.
